Common questions

The Lock Screen doesn't show my step.
Open Settings → Next Step and make sure Live Activities is on. Then start a step in the app.

Can I tap Done without opening the app?
Yes. Tap Done on the Lock Screen, or long-press the Dynamic Island. The next step appears right away.

What happens if a step takes longer than planned?
Nothing bad. The timer counts up and shows a gentle note. Take your time.

Can I use Siri or Shortcuts?
Yes. Say "Start next step in Next Step", or use the "Start Next Step" and "Add Task" actions in the Shortcuts app.

Is my data backed up or synced?
No. Everything stays on this iPhone, and we never receive it.
