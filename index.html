Privacy Policy

Next Step · Last updated October 20, 2026

Next Step does not collect, store, or share any personal data.

Your data stays on your iPhone

  • Your tasks and steps are stored only on your device.
  • The app has no accounts, analytics, advertising, or third-party SDKs.
  • The app does not make any network requests.
  • We can't see your tasks, and we never receive them.

Deleting your data

You can delete any task inside the app. If you delete the app, all of its data is removed from your device.

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
